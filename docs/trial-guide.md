# Veritas beta trial: guide for participants

Thank you for trying Veritas. It looks for signs that training data was tampered with or mislabelled. In this trial we
want to learn one thing: **are the items Veritas lists worth a person's time to look at?** Your marks answer that.

It takes about 30-45 minutes in total.

## 1. What to bring

One dataset of your own that you know reasonably well, in one of these forms:

| Kind | Format | Up to |
|---|---|---|
| Table | a CSV file with a label column | 200 MB, 200,000 rows |
| Images | a zip with one folder per class (`cat/`, `dog/`, ...) | 500 MB |
| Audio | a zip of WAV files in one folder per class | 500 MB |
| Video | a zip of video clips in one folder per class | 500 MB |

Best of all: data where you already know or suspect a few problems (wrong labels, odd files, duplicates). Please do
not upload personal data you are not allowed to share, or anything confidential.

If you have a part of the data you trust (for example last month's checked data), upload it as the **reference**:
Veritas then compares the rest against it and gives a clearer answer.

## 2. Check it

1. Sign in with the account we created for you, and turn on two-factor sign-in on the Account page if you like.
2. **Check data**: choose the kind, drop the file, pick the reference option, and start. A table takes seconds; a large
   image or video zip can take several minutes. You can leave the page; the result appears in **History**.
3. Read the result:
   - With a reference you get a verdict (how strong the evidence of tampering is) and a list of the items that drove it.
   - Without a reference you get ranked lists of the most unusual items per check, and no verdict.
   - The list is a starting point, not proof. Many listed items can be perfectly fine.

## 3. Mark the list (the important part)

For the **top 20 items** of the list (or all of them if there are fewer):

- Look at the item (the row in your file, or the image, clip or file name).
- Press **Real problem** if it is actually wrong (mislabelled, corrupted, a duplicate, something that should not be
  there), or **Fine** if it is ordinary data.
- Not sure? Leave it unmarked rather than guess.

Marks save at once and can be changed; press the same button again to clear it.

## 4. Tell us three things

Reply to the invitation email (or the feedback form we sent) with:

1. Overall, were the top 20 items **worth reviewing**? (yes / no, and why in a sentence)
2. Was there **something you expected Veritas to find and it did not**? Describe it.
3. Anything confusing in the app.

## 5. Your data

- Uploaded files are deleted as soon as the analysis finishes.
- The results and your marks are kept for 60 days, then deleted. You can delete them earlier on the Account page
  ("Delete my data"), or delete your whole account.
- Your marks are used only to measure how often listed items are real problems. We may publish counts and rates
  (for example "8 of 20 listed items were real problems"), never your data or file names.
