# Legal drafts shown in the app

`terms-of-service.md` and `privacy-policy.md` are verbatim copies of `docs/legal/`, which is the source.
They are drafts awaiting review (each says so at the top) and are shown in the app at `/terms` and `/privacy`
with a draft notice. When `docs/legal/` changes, copy the files here again; do not edit these copies.
